Park Aura

Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Park Aura ("we", "us") is an hourly parking marketplace. Drivers find and reserve parking spaces that fit their vehicle, and hosts list spaces they own or manage. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us in readable form.
  • Vehicle information: the vehicle name and body type you enter, used to estimate whether a space fits.
  • Listing information (hosts): space name, price, hours, dimensions, location, and photos you upload.
  • Reservations, messages and reviews: bookings you make or receive, messages between drivers and hosts, and ratings you leave.
  • Location: with your permission, your device's approximate or precise location is used to center the map. You can decline or turn this off in your device settings and still search by address.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. Card details go directly to Stripe and are not stored on our servers. We receive confirmation of a payment and its amount.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and show you spaces.
  • To process reservations and payments and notify you about bookings.
  • To let drivers and hosts message each other about a reservation.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Who we share information with

  • Other users: a host sees the driver's email and booking details for a reservation, and a driver sees the space's listing details. Listings, photos and reviews are visible to other users.
  • Service providers that run Park Aura: Supabase (database, login and file storage), Stripe (payments), Resend (email notifications), Vercel (website hosting), and mapping services (OpenStreetMap, and Apple Maps on iPhone). Addresses you search for are sent to a geocoding service to find them on the map.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

Retention and deleting your account

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time inside the app (the "Delete my account" link at the bottom of the home screen). Deleting your account permanently removes your account, your listed spaces, your reservations, reviews and messages. We may keep limited records where the law requires it, such as payment records held by Stripe. Deletion is blocked while you have an upcoming paid reservation, until it has passed.

Security

We use industry-standard providers and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Park Aura is intended for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above when we do.
